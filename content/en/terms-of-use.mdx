# GC Platform terms of use

## Your responsibilities

_For Canadian Digital Service (CDS) responsibilities, read GC Platform’s service level agreement._

We may suspend your account or service if you break these terms or misuse GC Platform or other CDS products.

Misuse includes not following these terms, terms of use of other CDS products, and not following your department or agency’s policies and procedures.

## Accept all conditions

- Review our policy documents to ensure you’re fully informed of your responsibilities and our processes, including uptime, security, and support. In addition to these terms of use, this includes our Privacy notice, Service level agreement and Accessibility statement.

## Practice continuous security, including security of API keys

- Use a valid government email only you can access, both to create your account and for 2-factor authentication (2FA).
- Do not share inboxes, account information, or passwords or store where others could find them. We do not permit and will deactivate shared inboxes. 
- If you leave your role, ensure you close or transfer to authorized staff any services you built with CDS products and access through GC Platform.  You must deal with information associated with those services following your department’s data handling policies. We may delete accounts and information linked to inactive email addresses.
- Report any security breach or vulnerability by using the [contact us page](https://design-system.canada.ca/en/contact/).
