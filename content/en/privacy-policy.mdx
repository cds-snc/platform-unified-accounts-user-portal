# Privacy Notice for staff using GC Platform

GC Platform is a product of the [Canadian Digital Service](https://digital.canada.ca/) (CDS), part of [Employment and Social Development Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development.html). Each CDS product and the [CDS website](https://digital.canada.ca/legal/privacy/) has its own privacy notice.

For now, Government of Canada staff can use GC Platform to login to GC Forms. Eventually, you’ll be able to use GC Platform for all CDS Platform products.

## We collect your personal information

We collect your name, work contact information, as well as your experience with and feedback on CDS Platform products. 

If you contact us or create an account, we use your information to reply or confirm that you’re authorized to use CDS Platform products, or provide you with information and support. We may also use your contact information to invite you to participate in research on CDS Platform products.

We also log Internet Protocol (IP) addresses. The IP address identifies the location and the internet/network provider of the computer or mobile device that you use to visit GC Platform. We use the IP logs to monitor GC Platform’s network and security. We do not link these logs with your name or email address. 

We hash your password and answers to security questions. Hashing ensures stored passwords and answers cannot be deciphered or reconstructed, even by CDS employees.

We’ll deactivate your account at your request. To make a request, [contact us](/contact-us). After deactivation, we keep your email address in our archives for two weeks and our audit logs for two years.

We collect information under [Department of Employment and Social Development Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/h-5.7/FullText.html) Section 5.1. You can read more about this in the Standard Personal Information Bank [PSU 915](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/information-about-programs-information-holdings/standard-personal-information-banks.html#psu915).  

## We may analyze your information using third-party software tools such as web analytics and artificial intelligence (AI)

From this analysis, we learn what to add, change, or improve. We also use this information to report on our impact.

Analytics show how people use our websites. By studying how people navigate, we can
improve GC Platform and all CDS Platform products.

We use Google Analytics for information about:

- Pages people visit and the links they select.
- Types of operating systems and browsers that visit GC Platform.
- Approximate location, if enabled on the browser.
- Date and time of visits.

We may also use AI to analyze feedback and support requests. When using third-party software, including analytics and AI, we remove any information that could identify you, such as your name and email address.

## We share some information

We may share general use case information about GC Platform in public forums, such as blog posts, presentations, and conferences.

If you use other CDS Platform products, we may share information with those services to improve your experience.

We use technology and services from other organizations. For example, to prevent digital attacks, we share information about when and how you use GC Platform with the [Canadian Centre for Cyber Security](https://cyber.gc.ca/en) (CCCS).

We also use open source software and services from the private sector. For the list of services, both public and private, visit Other Services used by GC Platform. 

We do not:

- Authorize other services to use your information for anything unrelated to either GC Platform or your other registered CDS Platform products.
- Sell or rent your personal information.
- Share your personal information for marketing purposes.

We may share information with law enforcement by court order.

## We protect your privacy

We handle your information under Part 4 of the [Department of Employment and Social Development Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/h-5.7/FullText.html#h-256161), the [Access to Information Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/a-1/page-1.html), and the [Privacy Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/ENG/ACTS/P-21/index.html).

We also follow Appendix E: Standard on Privacy in Web Analytics of the [Directive on Privacy Practices](https://www.tbs-sct.canada.ca/pol/doc-eng.aspx?id=18309). 

## You have privacy rights

The law requires that we protect your privacy. You have the right to access and review
your personal information. Use your GC Platform account to check, correct, or update
your information.

You also have the right to raise concerns about how we handle your personal information.
To learn more or to make a formal complaint, contact the [Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/for-individuals/).
