# GC Platform Service Level Agreement

## Our responsibilities
_For your responsibilities, read GC Platform’s terms of use._

### **GC Platform is a:**

- Pathway to sign into all CDS Platform products.
- Software as a Service (SaaS) system, built on open-source code from Zitadel, controlled and operated by the Canadian Digital Service (CDS).
- Cloud-hosted system using Amazon Web Services (AWS). 
- Medium integrity and medium availability service.

### **This agreement covers CDS responsibilities for GC Platform.**  It does not cover:

- Devices such as computers or mobile phones.
- Your other services or infrastructure, such as internet or email service providers.
- Services that we use for product infrastructure and features, including Amazon cloud services and [hCaptcha](https://www.hcaptcha.com/terms). We’re not responsible for problems, failures, outages or enforcing terms of other services.


### **CDS is responsible to**


**Maintain GC Platform’s security, including by**

1. Following the [AWS Shared Responsibility model](https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/shared-responsibility-model/). We’re responsible for securing areas assigned to the customer in the model, while AWS is responsible for infrastructure.
1. Patching security vulnerabilities in a timely manner, based on our determination of the level of threat. We patch major vulnerabilities as soon as possible and no more than a month after we find the root cause. Timelines may be longer for external dependencies for code. AWS is responsible for infrastructure patching. To learn more, read the [CDS Patch Management Guide](https://articles.alpha.canada.ca/cds-intranet-employee-guide/security-at-cds/patch-management-guideline/).
1. Configuring hCaptcha so that suspected threats and spam are blocked, but genuine users can access their accounts.
1. Responding quickly to incidents. GC Platform follows the [TBS Directive on Security Management](https://www.tbs-sct.canada.ca/pol/doc-eng.aspx?id=32611) and uses an Incident Response process to resolve issues in a timely way.
1. Providing on-call staff 24 hours a day7 days a week. We respond to Severity 1 incidents within 2 hours of detection.
1. Sharing application logs with the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (CCCS). GC Platform follows the [Direction on the Secure Use of Commercial Cloud Services: Security Policy Implementation Notice (SPIN)](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-government-innovations/cloud-services/direction-secure-use-commercial-cloud-services-spin.html).

**Provide, operate and support GC Platform by**

7. Hosting and supporting the GC Platform system.
8. Allowing authorized users to create accounts and sign into GC Platform so they can use CDS Platform products.
9. Providing reactive support services to monitor, track, and remedy issues.
10. Supporting requests and questions through GC Platform’s ‘contact us’ page. 

**Maintain timely operations by**

11. Committing to a service availability rate of 99.0%, excluding urgent scheduled maintenance and planned maintenance periods. Availability means the system is online with no performance issues. 
12. Ensuring GC Platform sends two-factor authentication in a timely way.
13. Guaranteeing 99.0% uptime, with no more than 1% downtime during regular business hours in a year. In contrast to availability, uptime means the system is online but could have performance issues. Our uptime guarantee excludes any downtime experienced by other services that GC Platform uses for product infrastructure and features, such as AWS.
14. Restoring the system to operation if there’s unplanned downtime. Downtime is an interruption in our ability to provide and operate GC Platform.
Unplanned downtime:
* Cannot be controlled by GC Platform.
* Triggers automatic alarms for our staff
* Includes interruptions outside of planned downtime, which may be due to work done during planned downtime.

**Inform you of service interruptions or discontinuation**

15. For planned downtime, we:
* Contact you at least three days in advance and minimize interference with your use.
* When possible, use weekends between 8:00 pm and 11:59 pm EST.
We cannot follow these steps for unplanned downtime.
16. For unplanned downtime, alert you as soon as we learn GC Platform is inaccessible for any reason, including issues with infrastructure providers.

For information on system and service interruptions, visit the [CDS system status page](https://status-statut.cds-snc.ca/).
